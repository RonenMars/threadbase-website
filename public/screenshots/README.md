# Focused landing assets

`mobile-session-needs-input.webp` is an authentic iPhone app capture with demonstration session data, not a recreated UI.
Source: `tb-mobile/artifacts/marketing/threadbase-maestro/01-start-anywhere-continue-anywhere/03-waiting-for-input.png`.
Capture provenance and scenario picks are documented in `tb-mobile/store-assets/tb-store-launch-plan.md` (captures dated 2026-10-06).
The image was resized proportionally from 1206 × 2622 to 810 × 1761 and encoded as WebP at quality 88; its contents were not changed or cropped.
The visible session is an English example, explicitly identified by the page caption in every locale.

`threadbase-icon.webp` is the existing app icon from `tb-mobile/assets/icon.png`, resized to 128 × 128 and encoded as WebP at quality 90.

These files are local, reusable assets; no external image or font service is used by the page.
